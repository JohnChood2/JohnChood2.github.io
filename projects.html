SPT-Pol AGN Monitoring

This project represents a breakthrough in millimeter wavelength astronomy, developing the first continuous high-cadence monitoring system for Active Galactic Nuclei using South Pole Telescope data. Our work has enabled unprecedented studies of AGN variability in the millimeter band.
